# Anderson Home Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 10, 2026**

Anderson Home is a private smart-lighting control application used to access and control an Anderson Home lighting controller.

## Information the app collects

The Anderson Home Android app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or third-party marketing services. The app does not sell personal information.

The app uses network access to communicate with the Anderson Home lighting system. As part of normal network communication, technical information may be processed, including IP address, HTTP request information, device/browser request information, session or authentication information used by the controller, lighting commands, and controller responses.

The app does not intentionally collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, call logs, SMS messages, payment information, health information, or advertising identifiers.

## How information is used

Information transmitted by the app is used only to connect to the Anderson Home lighting controller, maintain the application's control session, send lighting commands, receive controller status, and provide requested app functionality.

## Cloudflare

Remote access to the Anderson Home lighting controller may pass through Cloudflare infrastructure to provide HTTPS connectivity and network delivery. Cloudflare may process technical network information such as IP address and request metadata as part of providing its services. Cloudflare's handling of that information is governed by Cloudflare's own privacy policies.

## Data sharing

Anderson Home does not sell user data and does not share user data for advertising or marketing. Technical information may be processed by service providers, including Cloudflare, only as necessary to provide network connectivity, security, and application functionality.

## Data retention and deletion

The Android app does not maintain a separate remote user database. Local application data, such as WebView cookies or site storage used to maintain the controller session, may remain on the Android device until the app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Lighting settings and controller configuration may remain on the Anderson Home controller until they are changed, deleted, or the controller is reset. Technical logs retained by infrastructure providers are subject to those providers' retention practices.

## Security

Remote access is provided through an HTTPS endpoint. Reasonable measures are used to limit access to the controller and to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children's privacy

Anderson Home is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
