Silverado Stream Browser Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Silverado Stream Browser is a parked-use web browser designed for Android Automotive OS. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information We Collect

The developer of Silverado Stream Browser does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information from users of the app. The app does not include developer-operated advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Browsing Data

The app may store normal browser information on the vehicle itself, such as browsing history, website data, cookies, cached files, preferences, and bookmarks. This information is used to provide browser functionality and remains subject to the storage and security controls of Android Automotive OS and the embedded browser engine.

Websites and Streaming Services

When you visit a website or streaming service, information is exchanged directly with that third-party service as necessary to load and use the site. Those services may collect information under their own privacy policies, including login information, cookies, device or network information, and usage activity. Silverado Stream Browser does not control those third-party practices.

Permissions and Network Access

The app uses network access so you can browse websites and use supported web services. Any additional platform permissions are used only as required for browser operation on Android Automotive OS.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because the developer does not receive user browsing data, there is no developer-held personal data to retain or delete. Locally stored browser data can be removed by clearing the app's storage/data through the vehicle's Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Vehicle Safety

Silverado Stream Browser is intended for use while the vehicle is parked. It relies on Android Automotive OS and vehicle-level safety restrictions for availability while driving.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
